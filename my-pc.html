Privacy by design

The scanner binds only to 127.0.0.1 on your PC. It reads basic hardware fields and sends them to this browser over a temporary local connection. It does not collect usernames, serial numbers, personal files or installed-app lists. The report is saved only in this browser's local storage.

Important

This is the PC Hub local scanner, not the third-party System Requirements Lab executable. The third-party EXE cannot be redirected to PC Hub just by changing its download link.
